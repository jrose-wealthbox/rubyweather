  ---
  The engineering underneath, since you're checking the diction:

  - Five-lock couplet (the flagship): best/rest · write/fight · spec/tech(-debt)
  · red/bled · green/routine. Every stressed syllable in the line has a partner
  directly below it — that's the "scene of the crime / fiend of a rhyme"
  blueprint.
  - Four-line chains in the SolidQueue quatrain instead of two-line mirrors:
  polished / Solid / acknowledged / knowledge (that "AH-lidge" sound survives
  four lines), stacked on top of crew move / Queue's proof / resumes / room, on
  top of row / flow / tow / goes. Three sound-families running in parallel the
  whole quatrain — that's the Vomitspit move, where the chain outlives the
  couplet.
  - DOOM-grade absurd pairings: schema/femur and consumed/entombed are the kind
  of rhymes he'd reach for — semantically ridiculous, phonetically exact. (And
  "when it dreams, it recalls" is literally what WAL replay does after a crash,
  so the femur bar is also just... true.)
  - Diction cheats, per Rakim's license: crime/design is an eye-slant (aim/ain),
  and WAL wants to be pronounced "wall" for recalls to land — which is how most
  people say it anyway.

  The title's the thesis: a rhyme scheme is just referential integrity for
  syllables.

⏺ "GHOST METHODS"

  Metal face in the mist, one cut ruby at the eye-slit,
  caught a wild exception barehanded — no mitt,
  re-raised it ancient: StandardError lineage,
  Hammurabi probably pushed the first commit
  to diorite. Rollback? Try it.
  Riot.

  Alexandria never ran a pg_dump — whole library cooked,
  Cassandra told 'em twice, nobody hooked
  up the nightly job.
  Restore point: ash.
  Villain keep three replicas and an off-site stash.

  Swapped every plank on the Ship of Theseus, specs still green —
  ain't that genius? Same keel, new thesis,
  continuous integration, B.C. edition — believe this,
  while Sisyphus in the worker pool push a boulder up the cron,
  job rolls back at dawn, Zeus never mark it done:
  eternal retry. Exponential backoff for the gods.

  Breakfast: sip of GIN, slice of TOAST,
  keep it ACID — litmus for the low-dosed,
  dead tuples? Autovacuum in the back room,
  crumbs out the buffer cache. Snack food.

  Read replicas stretch — Reed Richards elastic,
  phantom reads is Sue Storm in your transaction (classic),
  Ben Grimm on migration day, clobberin' the schema,
  Johnny hotfixin' prod: flame on. Fever.

  After hours, Villain hold a séance: method_missing —
  ghost methods only answer when you ask with respect. Listen…
  respond_to_missing? Spooky action at a distance —
  Einstein hated it. Metaprogramming, minimal resistance.

  Et tu, Brute-force? Merged on the Ides,
  twenty-three commits in the back — "squash and merge," the Senate lied.
  Beware the March changelog.

  Ada Lovelace wrote loops for a machine that stayed vapor,
  Babbage never built it — pure faith in the paper,
  tests for a runtime strictly theoretical:
  her first commit predates the epoch. Negative timestamp.

  Never ask an object its class — impolite. Peep how it behave:
  if it quack under load, it's a duck. Wave.
  MINASWAN — Matz raised us nice, so we stay.
  And remember ALL CAPS when you spell the constant name:
  SCREAMING_SNAKE. It's a Ruby thing. Hold the shame.

  Back home in Latveria, containers clear the area,
  Kamal passed the barrier, health checks catch hysteria,
  logs quarantined like malaria —
  one majestic monolith, no microservice fiefdoms,
  every model in the kingdom rides a single deploy. Freedom?
  Doom decree it.

  Undefined method for nil: a nihilist koan. Sit still.
  &. — the lonely operator, safe-navigate the void.
  Villain perform_later.
  (See you later.)

  ---
  What changed under the hood:

  - Rubato, not couplets. Line lengths swing from sixteen-syllable gallops
  ("while Sisyphus in the worker pool push a boulder up the cron") to one-word
  snaps ("Riot."). The short lines land on a rhyme the long lines set up —
  that's DOOM letting the beat catch him.
  - Chains cross stanza borders now. The opening "ih-t" family (eye-slit / mitt 
  / commit / try it / riot) runs six lines through an enjambment; the -eria
  chain (Latveria / area / barrier / hysteria / malaria) stacks four deep the
  way "Rhymes Like Dimes" stacks its runs.
  - Association over argument. Nothing here claims Rails is great — the
  antiquity thread (Hammurabi → Alexandria → Theseus → Sisyphus) just dissolves
  into a breakfast bar, because GIN and TOAST are genuinely Postgres features
  (TOAST = The Oversized-Attribute Storage Technique) and DOOM never met a food
  pun he didn't keep.
  - The deep cuts are load-bearing: ghost methods is the real Ruby term for
  method_missing dispatch; Sue Storm is invisible, hence phantom reads; Caesar
  took twenty-three stab wounds, hence twenty-three commits; Ada's 1843 "commit"
  really would be a negative Unix timestamp; and the ALL CAPS bar flips the
  most famous DOOM line of all into SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE.
  - perform_later / "see you later" is the ActiveJob API closing the song — the
  villain doesn't exit, he just gets enqueued.
